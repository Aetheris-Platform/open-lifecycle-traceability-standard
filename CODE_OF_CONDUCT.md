# Code of Conduct

OLTS welcomes constructive participation from product engineering, systems engineering, QA, DevOps, security, compliance, ALM, MBSE, open-source, and standards communities.

## Expected Behavior

Participants are expected to:

- be respectful and professional;
- critique ideas, not people;
- assume good intent while still asking for evidence;
- acknowledge prior art and related standards;
- keep discussions focused on improving OLTS;
- avoid harassment, threats, discriminatory language, and personal attacks.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment or intimidation;
- discriminatory or demeaning language;
- sustained disruption of project discussions;
- publishing private information without consent;
- bad-faith participation intended to derail the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may moderate discussions, close issues, edit or remove comments where platform rules allow, or block participants who violate this code of conduct.

## Reporting

If you see behavior that violates this code, contact the maintainers through the repository owner or GitHub's reporting tools.
